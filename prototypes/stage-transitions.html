Hiring Stage Rules — Logic Prototype

Question: Do our stage-transition, confirmation, and undo rules behave sensibly when staff move applications normally, skip stages, make mistakes, or try something that should be blocked?

Throwaway prototype. Nothing is saved; reload to start over. Rules being tested:

  • Active stages: Applied → Screening → Interview → Final Review → Offer. Staff may move forward one stage or skip ahead (up to Offer).
  • Hired can only be reached from Offer, and is permanent — no undo.
  • Rejected and Withdrawn can be reached from any active stage.
  • Undo returns the application to the stage it actually came from (even after a skip). It works once — you can't undo twice in a row. It never works on Hired.
  • Moving backward is only possible through Undo.
  • Every move asks for confirmation first. Cancel changes nothing. Undo does not ask.
  • Editing candidate details never changes the stage or the undo.
